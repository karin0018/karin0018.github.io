About me

I am currently a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Professor Qi Liu . I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from USTC. My research focuses on knowledge-driven model reasoning, with a particular focus on education and medical reasoning.

I am also a visiting PhD student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), advised by Professor Dacheng Tao, since September 2026.
