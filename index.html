I am currently a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Professor Qi Liu . I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from USTC. My research focuses on knowledge-driven model reasoning, including Neural Language Processing, Knowledge Graph and LLM Agents.

Currently, I am a visiting student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), working with Prof. Dacheng Tao.
